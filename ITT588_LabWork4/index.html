OUR HERITAGE

Preserving Our Family Legacy

Roots & Legacy is an interactive family heritage website designed to preserve and explore family relationships.

Through a structured JSON dataset, the website dynamically displays family members, generations, parents, spouses, and children using JavaScript.

This project demonstrates the Model-View-Controller (MVC) architecture to organize data, presentation, and user interaction.
